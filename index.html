FAQ

Common questions

Which areas do you deliver to?

We are based in Kitale and serve Trans Nzoia and neighbouring counties. We can also arrange delivery further afield. Tell us your location in your request and we will confirm.

How quickly can I get a quotation?

Send us your list with quantities and specifications, and we will prepare a written quotation as quickly as possible. Clearer specifications mean faster and more accurate quotes.

Do you issue LPO-based supplies and invoices?

Yes. We work with Local Purchase Orders and issue delivery notes and invoices for every order, suited for institutional accounting.

Can you supply items not on your list?

Absolutely. Our core lines are listed, but if you need something else we will source it from trusted suppliers and quote for it.

How can I verify your company?

Our company number is PVT-DM1KB2V5, registered under the Companies Act, 2015. We can share our Certificate of Incorporation and the official Registrar of Companies search on request.
